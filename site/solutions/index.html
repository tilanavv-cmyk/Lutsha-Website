04
EdTech & e-learning services

Build, host and manage digital learning across the SADC region.

Through Edubyte Africa, Lutsha helps institutions and programme partners digitise learning, extend reach and deliver a consistent learner experience across locations.

Digital course development — learner guides, interactive content, video-ready structure and assessment activities. SCORM conversion — portable online learning packages for Moodle and compatible LMS environments. LMS hosting & setup — branded learning portals, course configuration and structured enrolment journeys. Managed e-learning — administration, learner support, reporting and continuous delivery oversight. Institutional content services — occupational, Report 191, NCV and professional short-course development.
