How it works

Six straightforward steps.

The application process should feel guided and transparent from the moment a learner arrives on the site.

01

Choose a programme

Review the available pathway, entry requirements, delivery model and current intake information.

02

Apply online

Complete the self-application form using a phone, tablet or computer.

03

Upload documents

Upload a copy of your ID, highest school certificate and proof of residence so the team can review your application.

04

Application review

Lutsha confirms eligibility, programme availability and any additional information required.

05

Quotation and acceptance

Approved applicants receive the relevant enrolment and fee information before registration is finalised.

06

Begin your journey

Attend orientation and follow the academic, practical and workplace learning plan for your programme.
