What is LaunchLab?

More than training.
A working launchpad.

LaunchLab helps people move from learning a skill to building an enterprise around it.

Participants work through a practical plan with a mentor, apply each lesson to their own product or service, collect evidence of progress and prepare for real customers, partnerships and suitable growth opportunities.

It is not a once-off workshop or a promise of funding. It is a structured development process designed to help entrepreneurs become more capable, more credible and better prepared.

Three tracks. One enterprise.

Develop the person, the offer and the business together.

Each workstream runs alongside the others so that practical progress and enterprise progress stay connected.

Track 01

Technical development

Improve practical skill, quality, consistency and the products or services that make the enterprise valuable.

  • Technical coaching
  • Quality standards
  • Portfolio and evidence
Track 02

Business development

Build a viable model around real customers, workable pricing, stronger operations and a route to market.

  • Business model and goals
  • Costing, pricing and cash flow
  • Branding, sales and customers
Track 03

Funding readiness

Prepare the documents, plans, quotations and evidence needed to pursue appropriate funding opportunities.

  • Readiness assessment
  • Document preparation
  • Opportunity matching and follow-up

The 12-month journey

Every month moves the enterprise forward.

Participants begin with a baseline and individual development plan. From there, learning, application, mentor feedback and evidence-building follow a repeatable monthly rhythm.

Week 1 Learn & demonstrateWeek 2 Apply to the businessWeek 3 Mentor & correctWeek 4 Review & prepare
  1. Months 1–2Discover & define

    Baseline assessment, individual goals, technical plan and business model.

  2. Months 3–4Prepare & price

    Products, services, customers, costing, suppliers and the funding-readiness file.

  3. Months 5–7Formalise & reach

    Compliance, brand, marketing tools, digital presence and customer activity.

  4. Months 8–9Plan & apply

    Business planning, financial projections, pitches and suitable applications.

  5. Months 10–12Trade & grow

    Market exposure, targeted coaching, final portfolio and the next growth plan.

Funding readiness

Prepare well before an opportunity opens.

Participants are supported to organise the core business and personal documents commonly requested by funding programmes, clarify what support the business needs and prepare credible plans and quotations.

Important: Lutsha may assist eligible participants to prepare and submit suitable applications. Funding decisions remain with the relevant funder and funding is not guaranteed.

Questions, answered

Before you apply or partner with us.

Is LaunchLab only for people who already have a business?

No. Cohort criteria may differ, but LaunchLab can support people refining an early business idea as well as owners of existing enterprises ready to strengthen or grow.

Does joining LaunchLab guarantee business funding?

No. LaunchLab provides funding-readiness and application support where appropriate. Every funding decision is made independently by the relevant funder.

Will every cohort follow exactly the same programme?

The core enterprise journey stays consistent, while technical activities, mentors, market exposure and milestones are adapted to the sector and the specific cohort.

Can an organisation sponsor or host a cohort?

Yes. LaunchLab can be discussed with municipalities, corporates, funders, industry partners and community organisations seeking a structured enterprise-development model.
