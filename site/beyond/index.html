OUR PURPOSE

International opportunity.
African purpose.

Your qualification can take you further than the classroom.

Lutsha Beyond is Lutsha Institute of Professional Learning’s internationalisation platform. It connects South African occupational learners with global knowledge, work-based learning, cultural intelligence and future-facing practice.

It also develops reciprocal pathways for international partners to engage with African skills and innovation. The aim is to bring new perspectives home—and turn learning into value for workplaces and communities.

BEFORE YOU TAKE THE NEXT STEP

Your questions,
answered.

Is international travel included in my course?

Enrolling in a Lutsha course does not automatically include travel, funding or an international placement. Beyond opportunities have their own arrangements and participation requirements.

Does every opportunity involve travelling?

No. Beyond Classroom includes virtual global learning, international masterclasses and collaborative projects.

Where can I find destinations, dates and costs?

Contact Lutsha for confirmed opportunities and their specific details. Destinations, hosts, funding and dates depend on the arrangements for each opportunity.

Can an institution propose a collaboration?

Yes. Email beyond@lutsha.org.za with your organisation, area of interest and proposed form of collaboration.
