Programme fees

Prices are stated in South African rand (ZAR). The fee displayed on the programme page or checkout at the time of purchase is the fee that applies to that transaction.

Where the online catalogue, a written quotation or checkout displays a different current price, that displayed price will apply. Please contact us before paying if anything is unclear.

What the fee covers

Unless the programme page says otherwise, the fee covers enrolment, access to the specified learning content and learning activities, and the assessment or completion process described for that programme. Optional items, travel, accommodation, connectivity, devices, external examination fees or replacement certificates are excluded unless expressly stated.

Payment

  • Payment is made using the methods offered at checkout, including Paystack where available.
  • Enrolment or access is confirmed only after payment is successfully received, unless Lutsha agrees to another arrangement in writing.
  • Any instalment plan, discount, bursary or sponsored arrangement applies only when confirmed in writing and may have additional conditions.
  • Lutsha may correct an obvious pricing error before accepting an enrolment. If payment has already been taken, the learner may accept the correct price or receive a full refund.

Receipts and tax

A payment confirmation or receipt will be issued electronically. Any applicable tax treatment will be shown on the quotation, invoice or checkout. Learners should retain their payment confirmation.

Pricing questions

Email study@lutsha.org.za or call 073 178 9245 before payment if you need a written quotation or clarification.
