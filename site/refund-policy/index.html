Refund policy

How refunds are assessed and processed for Lutsha programme purchases.

Last updated: 18 September 2026
PoliciesPricingRefund policyCancellation policyTerms of service

Scope and statutory rights

This policy applies to fees paid directly to Lutsha Empowerment (Pty) Ltd, trading as Lutsha Institute of Professional Learning, through our online channels. Nothing in this policy limits a learner’s rights under applicable South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

When a refund may be available

  • A duplicate or incorrect payment was processed.
  • Lutsha cancels a programme and cannot offer an acceptable alternative.
  • A learner validly exercises an applicable statutory cooling-off or cancellation right.
  • A learner withdraws before a programme starts, subject to any reasonable, disclosed administration costs or non-recoverable third-party charges.
  • Exceptional circumstances are supported by reasonable documentation and Lutsha approves the request.

After a programme has started or digital content has been accessed, any refund will be assessed fairly in light of the services already provided, materials accessed, costs incurred and applicable law. No clause in this policy removes a remedy available for defective, misrepresented or undelivered services.

How to request a refund

Email study@lutsha.org.za with the learner’s full name, programme, payment date, amount, transaction reference, reason for the request and supporting documents. Requests should be sent as soon as reasonably possible.

Processing times

  1. We aim to acknowledge a complete request within two working days.
  2. We aim to communicate a decision within ten working days, although complex cases may take longer.
  3. Approved refunds are normally submitted within fifteen working days of approval.

Bank and payment-provider processing times may affect when the funds appear. Refunds are ordinarily returned to the original payment method and payer. Where this is not possible, identity and banking verification may be required.

Deductions and non-refundable amounts

Any deduction will be reasonable, permitted by law and explained to the learner. It may include the value of services already delivered, non-returnable learning materials, or non-recoverable third-party charges disclosed before purchase. Payment processing fees will only be deducted where lawful and genuinely non-recoverable.

Review of a decision

If you disagree with a decision, request an internal review within five working days and explain the basis of the review. A person not responsible for the original decision will consider it where reasonably practicable.

Refund support

Send refund requests to study@lutsha.org.za. Include the payment reference so we can locate the transaction quickly.
