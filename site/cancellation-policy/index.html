Cancellation policy

Rules for learner cancellations, withdrawals, transfers and changes made by Lutsha.

Last updated: 18 September 2026
PoliciesPricingRefund policyCancellation policyTerms of service

Learner cancellation or withdrawal

To cancel an enrolment or withdraw from a programme, email study@lutsha.org.za. State the learner’s full name, programme, transaction or application reference, reason and requested effective date. A request is effective when received by Lutsha and acknowledged in writing.

Before the programme starts

A learner may cancel before the published start date or before access to on-demand digital content is provided. Refund eligibility is determined under our Refund Policy and applicable law. Lutsha may offer a transfer, credit or deferment as an alternative, but the learner is not required to accept an alternative where the law provides a refund right.

After commencement or access

Once teaching has started, a session has been attended, learning materials have been issued or digital content has been accessed, the learner may still withdraw. Fees for services already supplied and reasonable costs incurred may remain payable, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the case.

Transfers and deferments

A request to transfer to another programme, cohort or date is subject to availability, academic suitability and any price difference. A deferment must be approved in writing and may expire if the learner does not use it within the stated period. Credits and enrolments may not be transferred to another person without Lutsha’s written approval.

Changes or cancellation by Lutsha

Lutsha may change a venue, timetable, facilitator, delivery method or start date where reasonably necessary. We will give affected learners reasonable notice and aim to provide a substantially equivalent learning experience.

If Lutsha cancels a programme, or makes a material change that the learner reasonably cannot accept, the learner may choose an available alternative, a credit, or a refund of fees paid for the undelivered portion. Lutsha is not responsible for indirect costs such as travel or accommodation unless required by law or expressly agreed in writing.

Failure to attend

Not attending or not logging in does not by itself cancel an enrolment. The learner must submit a written request. Repeated absence or failure to meet programme requirements may result in withdrawal in accordance with programme rules, after reasonable communication with the learner.

Cancellation support

Email study@lutsha.org.za as early as possible so we can explain the available options.
